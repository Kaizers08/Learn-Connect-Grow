Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Edtech Mentoring ("the Platform", "we", "us") is an academic capstone project developed to connect mentees with approved mentors in an online mentoring environment. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

01

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: First name, last name, email address, and password (or Google account details).
  • Profile Information: Your role, university/company, job title, expertise, skills, photo, date of birth, country, phone number, and gender.
  • Mentor Verification Documents: Certifications and diploma, used solely for admin approval.
  • Communication Data: Messages via the chat feature and AI chatbot conversations.
  • Usage Data: Session bookings, learning material progress, and feedback or ratings submitted.
02

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To match mentees with suitable mentors based on shared interests and expertise.
  • To enable communication between mentors and mentees.
  • To track learning progress and display completion status.
  • To allow mentees to leave feedback and ratings for mentors.
  • To verify and approve mentor applications.
03

Third-Party Services

Our AI chatbot uses a third-party provider (Groq) to generate responses. Messages sent to the chatbot are transmitted to this service. We do not control how Groq processes data beyond generating the response.

04

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Supabase, a secure database and authentication provider. We take reasonable measures to protect your information.

05

Your Rights

You may access, edit, or delete your profile information at any time through Settings. Account deletion removes your personal data from our system.

06

Data Retention

Data is retained while your account is active. Upon deletion, personal data is removed. Some anonymized data may be retained for academic evaluation purposes.

07

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

08

Contact Us

Questions? Contact the development team at contact@edtechmentoring.com.
