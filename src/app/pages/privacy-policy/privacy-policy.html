Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Edtech Mentoring ("the Platform", "we", "us") is an academic capstone project developed to connect mentees with approved mentors in an online mentoring environment. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform.

01

Information We Collect

  • Account Information First name, last name, email address, and password (or Google account details if you sign in with Google).
  • Profile Information Your role (mentee or mentor), university/company, job title, area of expertise, technical skills, photo, date of birth, country, phone number, and gender.
  • Mentor Verification Documents Certifications and diploma (for mentor applicants only), used solely for admin approval purposes.
  • Communication Data Messages exchanged through the platform's chat feature, and conversations with the AI chatbot assistant.
  • Usage Data Session bookings, learning material progress, and feedback or ratings you submit.
02

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To match mentees with suitable mentors based on shared interests and expertise.
  • To enable communication between mentors and mentees.
  • To track learning progress and display completion status.
  • To allow mentees to leave feedback and ratings for mentors.
  • To verify and approve mentor applications.
03

Third-Party Services

Our AI chatbot assistant uses a third-party AI service provider (Groq) to generate responses to your questions. When you send a message to the chatbot, that message is transmitted to this third-party service in order to generate a reply. We do not control how this third party processes data beyond generating the response.

04

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Supabase, a secure database and authentication provider. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, though no online system can guarantee complete security.

05

Your Rights

You may access, edit, or delete your profile information at any time through your account Settings. You may also request account deletion, which will remove your personal data from our system.

06

Data Retention

Your information is retained for as long as your account remains active. Upon account deletion, your personal data will be removed, though some anonymized or aggregated data may be retained for academic evaluation purposes related to this capstone project.

07

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

08

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the development team at contact@edtechmentoring.com.
